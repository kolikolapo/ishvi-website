Craft Brewery & Beer Garden!

ISHVI — For us, it's not just a name. It's the feeling of finding new energy after a long day with premium craft beer, good conversation, and music.

We brew with true craft principles: time, care, and without compromise. Every batch is an experiment and a new story.

ISHVI is more than a brewery. It's an atmosphere — an open beer garden where unique taste and a cozy environment create perfect harmony.
