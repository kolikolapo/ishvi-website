Our Beer Collection

Classics & Experiments

Great emotion crafted in small batches.

We don't brew a lot, we brew the best. Each batch is small, ensuring absolute quality control and uncompromising taste.

Lager

ISHVI LAGER I

ABV 5%
IBU 17
OG 12°

This lager combines classic crispness with modern craft balance. The result is a light, crystal-clear, and harmonious beer that reveals its gentle character from the very first sip. Nothing extra here — just selected malts, carefully balanced aromatics, and a purity that unfolds naturally in every drop. Its light body and delicate floral notes perfectly complement hot summer days.

Lager

ISHVI LAGER II

ABV 5%
IBU 13
OG 12°

This lager merges classic German precision with modern craft philosophy. The result is an elegant, viscous, and lively beer that reveals its true character instantly. Nothing superfluous - just precisely selected ingredients, a time- and temperature-controlled process, and a taste that naturally opens up in every sip.

New England IPA

ISHVI NEIPA

ABV 6.8%
IBU 50
OG 17°

ISHVI NEIPA is built on the idea that beer can be a true aromatic experience. Intense tropical fruit flavors, bright citrus notes, and a velvety texture intertwine so that a new layer unfolds with every sip. It is precisely this balance and aromatic depth that create the special character making ISHVI NEIPA truly exceptional.

Belgian Witbier

ISHVI WIT

ABV 5%
IBU 14
OG 13°

Wheat beer is our pride. It is ideal for those who appreciate balance, freshness, and the authenticity of true craft. Our wheat beer is designed so you feel the smoothness, coolness, and aromatic harmony in every sip. The soft texture of wheat, the natural fruity tones of yeast, and a perfectly balanced structure create a beer that is both light and full of character.

Oatmeal Stout

ISHVI STOUT

ABV 6.8%
IBU 20
OG 16.5°

This is a beer whose character gradually unfolds in every sip - first with deep aromas of coffee and cocoa, and then with a velvety texture created by nitrogen micro-bubbles. Our stout is crafted so that every sip is a calm, rich, and impressive experience - combining depth and elegance simultaneously.
