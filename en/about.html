History of the Name

What does ISHVI mean?

It means rebirth. Like the feeling of rebirth you experience when drinking a good beer. For us, this word is not just a name - it is the birth of an idea that is changing craft culture.

ISHVI didn't start with the question "how much beer should we sell". ISHVI started with the question - what kind of beer would you truly enjoy drinking? For us, beer is not just a product. It is a high-tech process, time, and responsibility.

We believe that great taste is not created in a rush. It is shaped by carefully selected ingredients - malt, hops, water, and most importantly: technological precision and patience.

ISHVI is not a mass-market beer, nor does it try to be. We brew beer for those who appreciate it.

Cultural Traits

Historical Roots

Georgia is the homeland of wine, but the tradition of brewing beer in the mountains dates back centuries. We combine these ancient methods with modern craft technologies.

Unique Character

We create a product that has no equivalent in the local market. It is a synthesis of international quality and Georgian character — a beer that does not imitate any other.

Why Craft and not Mass-Market?

Handcrafted quality instead of industrial standards!

Today, beer is a culture that unites people, ideas, and stories around a single table.

ISHVI embodies this exact philosophy. It means rebirth. Like the feeling of revival experienced when enjoying a perfect pint. It is a return to the roots, but with a modern vision. Respect for history and the courage to create entirely new tastes.

We respect Bavarian precision, Czech culture, British traditions, and American audacity, but we blend all of this with our own Georgian soul. ISHVI is a place where global craft beer culture begins a new story. Our beer is not brewed for mass production - it is brewed for people who value authenticity and look for a story in every glass.

"For us, beer is an experience you drink. A history you feel. A taste you remember!"
