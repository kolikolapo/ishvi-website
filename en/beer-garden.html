BEER KITCHEN

Our kitchen was built around premium craft beer. Here, Austrian and European classics meet Georgian flavours: Munich and Viennese sausages, Georgian kupati, Skhvababi, our signature smoked pork ribs and much more. Every dish is crafted to pair with ISHVI’s craft beers. At our bar, you’ll find exclusive cocktails made with our very own craft beers.
